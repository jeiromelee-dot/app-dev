
# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is one of my favorite series because it combines mystery, science fiction, horror, and adventure. The story follows a group of friends who encounter supernatural events in their hometown.

### Why I Like It
- Interesting and exciting storyline.
- Amazing characters and friendships.
- Unexpected twists and mysteries.
- Great visual effects and music.

### My Favorite Character
**Eleven** – I like her because she is brave, powerful, and willing to protect her friends.

### Rating
**10/10** – I highly recommend this series to people who enjoy mystery and adventure.

> "Friends don't lie."
